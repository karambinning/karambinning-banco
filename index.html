Walk-in welcome
Building no. 15

From counting-house to café.

"Banco" is the old word for bank.

Number 15 St Sepulchre Gate has been many things — but for most of its life, it was where Doncaster came to deposit, withdraw, and quietly sign for things on heavy paper. The ledgers are gone. The high ceilings, the cornicing, and the architectural details that made the building feel important in the first place are not.

We kept what we could and added what a café needs: long tables for slow brunches, a counter that smells of coffee, and a bar that comes alive in the late afternoon. The result is a room that still feels a bit ceremonial — only now you can order pancakes in it.

It's a place to bring your mum, your dog, your boss, and your worst friend, in roughly that order. Doncaster, DN1. Permanent fixtures since the day we opened.
